Mutation

Section titled “Mutation”
Field Type Description
acceptTerms
input: TermsAcceptInput!		 TermsAcceptPayload Records that the signed-in user agreed to the terms of use, which forbid objectionable content and abuse. The apps ask before anyone chats.
addPhotographer
input: PhotographerAddInput!		 PhotographerAddPayload Grants a photographer’s hedonism_bot account access to the audience, and issues the token it publishes photos with. The token is shown only here: the photographer pastes it into hedonism_bot.
banAudienceMember
input: AudienceMemberBanInput!		 AudienceMemberBanPayload Kicks a member and keeps them out: they can’t sign in on the audience or chat in its venues until unbanned.
blockUser
input: BlockedUserAddInput!		 BlockedUserAddPayload Blocks someone: chat rooms stop showing the signed-in user that person’s messages and presence. They aren’t told.
buyCover
input: CoverBuyInput!		 CoverBuyPayload Starts paying tonight’s cover at a venue, through the venue’s provider. Stripe: finish the payment with Stripe’s payment sheet (paymentIntentClientSecret, on the venue’s account stripeAccountId, with publishableKey), then call confirmCover. Square: make a payment token with Square’s In-App Payments SDK (squareApplicationId, squareLocationId), then call payCover with it. Asking again the same night returns the same pass.
clearChatRoom
input: ChatRoomClearInput!		 ChatRoomClearPayload Clears a venue’s or a locale’s chat room: every message in it is removed, for everyone in the room and from the transcript.
confirmCover
input: CoverConfirmInput!		 CoverConfirmPayload Checks a cover payment with its provider after the app finishes paying, so the pass works before the webhook arrives.
connectEventbrite
input: EventbriteConnectInput!		 EventbriteConnectPayload Starts connecting an organizer’s Eventbrite account. Send the organizer to authorizeUrl; after they approve, Eventbrite returns them to returnTo with eventbrite=connected (or denied, or error) and their upcoming events are imported.
connectVenueSquare
input: VenueSquareConnectInput!		 VenueSquareConnectPayload Starts connecting a venue’s Square account so it can take cover in the apps. Send its manager to authorizeUrl; Square brings them back to returnTo with square=connected, pick_location (pick one with setVenueSquareLocation), declined or error.
connectVenueStripe
input: VenueStripeConnectInput!		 VenueStripeConnectPayload Starts connecting a venue’s Stripe account so it can take cover in the apps. Send its manager to onboardingUrl; Stripe brings them back to returnTo with stripe=connected (or pending, or error).
createEvent
input: EventCreateInput!		 EventCreatePayload Adds an event at one of the audience’s venues.
createEventSeries
input: EventSeriesCreateInput!		 EventSeriesCreatePayload Adds a recurring series run by one of the audience’s venues or people, and puts its occurrences on the calendar.
createLocale
input: LocaleCreateInput!		 LocaleCreatePayload Adds a locale to the audience: a city or neighborhood its venues are in.
createPerson
input: PersonCreateInput!		 PersonCreatePayload Adds talent by name, before they have a Facebook Page. Link one later with linkPersonFacebook.
createTag
input: TagCreateInput!		 TagCreatePayload Adds a tag to the audience’s list.
createVenue
input: VenueCreateInput!		 VenueCreatePayload Adds a venue to the audience: a bar, club or other place that holds events.
deleteAccount
input: AccountDeleteInput!		 AccountDeletePayload Deletes the signed-in user’s account and everything recorded about them (see UserDataEraser), and signs them out everywhere. It can’t be undone.
deleteEvent
input: EventDeleteInput!		 EventDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s events, with its RSVPs. Canceling it keeps it listed instead. Deleting an occurrence of a series skips its date.
deleteEventSeries
input: EventSeriesDeleteInput!		 EventSeriesDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s recurring series with its upcoming occurrences, except ones edited on their own or that people have RSVPed to. Past occurrences stay as ordinary events.
deleteLocale
input: LocaleDeleteInput!		 LocaleDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s locales. Its venues must be moved or deleted first.
deletePerson
input: PersonDeleteInput!		 PersonDeletePayload Removes a person from the audience. Their Facebook Page stays known to AudienceKit.
deleteTag
input: TagDeleteInput!		 TagDeletePayload Removes one of the audience’s tags, and takes it off everything it was applied to.
deleteVenue
input: VenueDeleteInput!		 VenueDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s venues and its events. Hiding it keeps them instead.
disconnectTicketingAccount
input: TicketingAccountDisconnectInput!		 TicketingAccountDisconnectPayload Disconnects a ticketing account and stops its webhook. Events imported from it stay.
endPing
input: PingEndInput!		 PingEndPayload Ends the signed-in user’s ping.
hideEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoHideInput!		 EventPhotoHidePayload Hides a photographer’s photo from the apps in this audience. The photographer publishing it again doesn’t bring it back; showing it does.
identifyWithTribe
input: TribeIdentifyInput!		 TribeIdentifyPayload Identifies the signed-in user with one of the audience’s tribes, or changes whether that’s private. Private is the default: only people in the same tribe see it.
issueVenueDevice
input: VenueDeviceIssueInput!		 VenueDeviceIssuePayload Issues a one-time device code with permissions for this venue.
joinPing
input: PingJoinInput!		 PingJoinPayload Says the signed-in user is in for a ping, for one pick or (with no target) the whole ping.
kickAudienceMember
input: AudienceMemberKickInput!		 AudienceMemberKickPayload Signs a member out of the audience everywhere and drops them from any chat they have open. They can sign in again.
leavePing
input: PingLeaveInput!		 PingLeavePayload Takes the signed-in user out of a ping they’re in.
leaveTribe
input: TribeLeaveInput!		 TribeLeavePayload Removes the signed-in user from one of the tribes they identify with. Nothing is kept.
linkPersonFacebook
input: PersonLinkFacebookInput!		 PersonLinkFacebookPayload Links talent to a Facebook Page or profile, adding the person when no personId is given. People added by name with createPerson are linked here once their Page is known.
matchVenueTicketmaster
input: VenueMatchTicketmasterInput!		 VenueMatchTicketmasterPayload Links a venue to its Ticketmaster venue, so the scheduled import reads its Ticketmaster events. Without ticketmasterVenueId, looks it up by name near the venue.
moderateVenueChat
input: VenueChatModerateInput!		 VenueChatModeratePayload Kicks, bans or unbans a person in one venue chat, without changing their audience access.
payCover
input: CoverPayInput!		 CoverPayPayload Pays a Square venue’s cover with a payment token from Square’s In-App Payments SDK (a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay), after buyCover. The pass works as soon as this returns it paid.
pinChatMessage
input: ChatMessagePinInput!		 ChatMessagePinPayload Pins an announcement under its chat room’s title, replacing the one pinned there, or unpins it.
pushEventToEventbrite
input: EventPushToEventbriteInput!		 EventPushToEventbritePayload Puts an event created in the console on Eventbrite now: a draft the first time, updated after that. With publish, it goes public too, once it has tickets on Eventbrite; the organizer’s “Add to Facebook” setting on Eventbrite then puts it on their Facebook Page.
refreshPage
input: PageRefreshInput!		 PageRefreshPayload Refreshes a venue’s or person’s Page from Facebook now instead of waiting for the scheduled refresh.
refundAdmission
input: AdmissionRefundInput!		 AdmissionRefundPayload Refunds a cover. Buyers can refund their own until it’s scanned at the door or the night is over; the venue’s door staff and the audience’s admins can refund any paid cover.
registerDevice
input: DeviceRegisterInput!		 DeviceRegisterPayload Stores the push notification token for the device this session signed in on.
removeChatMessage
input: ChatMessageRemoveInput!		 ChatMessageRemovePayload Removes a message from its chat room. Everyone in the room stops seeing it at once, and it no longer shows in the transcript.
removeMyChatMessage
input: ChatMessageRemoveOwnInput!		 ChatMessageRemoveOwnPayload Removes one of your own messages from its chat room. Everyone in the room stops seeing it at once.
reportChatMessage
input: ChatMessageReportInput!		 ChatMessageReportPayload Reports a message in a venue’s or locale’s chat room to the audience’s admins, who see it in the console’s activity log and can kick or ban the sender. Optionally blocks the sender too.
reportEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoReportInput!		 EventPhotoReportPayload Reports a photographer’s photo, for example a photo of yourself you want taken down. It’s hidden at once, until the audience’s admins look at it; the photographer sees it was reported.
reportLocation
input: LocationReportInput!		 LocationReportPayload Records the user’s position and returns the venue they’re in, or what’s near them.
resetAudienceInvite
input: AudienceInviteResetInput!		 AudienceInviteResetPayload Makes a new invite link for the audience. The old link stops working; people who already joined keep their listings.
resetPhotographerToken
input: PhotographerTokenResetInput!		 PhotographerTokenResetPayload Issues a photographer a new token. Their old one stops working, so they paste the new one into hedonism_bot.
revokeVenueDevice
input: VenueDeviceRevokeInput!		 VenueDeviceRevokePayload Revokes a venue device and its unused code.
rsvpEvent
input: EventRSVPInput!		 EventRSVPPayload Sets the user’s RSVP to an event in AudienceKit. It isn’t sent to Facebook, which no longer lets apps RSVP for people.
scanAdmission
input: AdmissionScanInput!		 AdmissionScanPayload Checks a pass’s QR code at a venue’s door. The first scan of a paid pass checks them in; later ones that night are re-entry.
sendChatMessage
input: ChatMessageSendInput!		 ChatMessageSendPayload Posts to a venue’s or a locale’s chat room, as the signed-in admin (STAFF) or as the venue (VENUE). Everyone in the room sees it at once. Admins post as either; venue listing managers only as their venue.
sendPing
input: PingSendInput!		 PingSendPayload Tells friends the signed-in user wants to go out tonight, naming venues or events, or neither.
setAudienceMemberDoor
input: AudienceMemberDoorSetInput!		 AudienceMemberDoorSetPayload Lets a member scan cover passes at every venue’s door in the audience, or stops them.
setPersonLocale
input: PersonLocaleSetInput!		 PersonLocaleSetPayload Features a person in one of the audience’s locales, or stops featuring them there.
setVenueSquareLocation
input: VenueSquareLocationSetInput!		 VenueSquareLocationSetPayload Picks which of the venue’s Square locations its cover is paid to.
showEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoShowInput!		 EventPhotoShowPayload Shows a hidden or reported photo in the apps again.
startMaintenanceRun
input: MaintenanceRunStartInput!		 MaintenanceRunStartPayload Starts a maintenance operation on the worker. On an audience’s endpoint it runs for that audience; on the tenants endpoint it runs for audienceId, or platform-wide without one.
unbanAudienceMember
input: AudienceMemberUnbanInput!		 AudienceMemberUnbanPayload Lets a banned member sign in and chat again.
unblockUser
input: BlockedUserRemoveInput!		 BlockedUserRemovePayload Unblocks someone the signed-in user blocked.
updateAudience
input: AudienceUpdateInput!		 AudienceUpdatePayload Renames an audience, changes its subdomain, links its Facebook Page or sets the App Review period its app. Subdomains and Pages change only from /v1/tenants/graphql.
updateAudienceTheme
input: AudienceThemeUpdateInput!		 AudienceThemeUpdatePayload Replaces an audience’s theme: its design system preset, tagline and color overrides.
updateEvent
input: EventUpdateInput!		 EventUpdatePayload Changes one of the audience’s events.
updateEventSeries
input: EventSeriesUpdateInput!		 EventSeriesUpdatePayload Changes one of the audience’s recurring series. Its upcoming occurrences follow, except ones edited on their own.
updateLocale
